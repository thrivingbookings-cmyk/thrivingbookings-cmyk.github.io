Courses Offered

Select a category to view our comprehensive training options.

MCA STCW Basic Safety Training

Mandatory internationally recognised safety certification required for all working seafarers.

MCA PDSD

Proficiency in Designated Security Duties – required security training for crew on commercial vessels.

MCA Yacht Rating

Entry-level superyacht certification recognised by the Maritime & Coastguard Agency for deck crew.

IYT RIB Training

Rigid Inflatable Boat training for powerboat handling and crew tender operations.

EDH

Efficient Deck Hand qualification – essential for deck crew.

AEC 1

Approved Engine Course 1 – for those starting a career in engineering.

AEC 2

Approved Engine Course 2 – to further develop engineering knowledge.

ECDIS

Electronic Chart Display & Information System.

IYT Master of Yachts 200T

International qualifications (Limited & Unlimited) up to and over 200 gross tons.

Barista Training

Professional coffee preparation, milk texturing, and customer service.

Ideal for: Stewards/esses, Café Staff

Food Safety Level 2

Highfield UK Approved. Worldwide recognized food hygiene and safety practices.

Ideal for: Galley Crew, Hospitality

Food Safety Level 3

For supervisory roles requiring deep food safety management understanding.

Ideal for: Chefs, Managers
